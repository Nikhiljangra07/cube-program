# RUNBOOK 9B — the aligned-target foresight face (Qwen base, bench-verbatim gate)

**Question:** run 9 isolated the poison — every corpus-side instrument measured a
self-authored "foresight-as-depth" definition while the bench measures
"foresight-as-calibration" (a realistic reaction a move or two ahead, no over-reach).
9b removes the poison and takes the properly-aimed shot: same problems, same base
(Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507, qualified in run 9), same recipe — but the corpus is
generated FOR the bench definition and admitted BY the bench judge itself.

**Definition (Nikhil, 2026-07-26, adopted as the design center):** foresight =
adversarial anticipation — "assume my opponent's move and make plans around it; be
prepared for the actions." This matches the bench rubric ("anticipate a realistic
downstream reaction a move or two ahead — WITHOUT over-reach") — anticipation and
pre-positioning, not depth-prophecy.

**Program law (new, permanent, earned by runs 8-9):** no instrument may define a lane
in its own words. The verdict instrument is the single source of truth — quoted,
never paraphrased. The 9b admission gate IS `sonnet_judge` (the bench judge prompt,
byte-identical import), not a re-worded cousin.

## DUAL-EVAL DESIGN (Nikhil, 2026-07-26 — "two evals, both satisfied")

**EVAL A — the frozen v5 bench (unchanged, byte-frozen).** 48 evidence-THIN problems,
sonnet_judge, 6 dims. Measures calibration under thin evidence + guards overall
regression. Keeps nine runs of comparability. NOT broken — incomplete for the
adversarial-anticipation facet, which is why B exists.

**EVAL B — the DOSSIER bench (new instrument, frozen HERE, before any face output
exists).** Measures Nikhil's foundation: evidence-fed opponent modeling — "collaborate
on the surface, map their actions, predict their moves from the data you already got,
plan around it." Depth is licensed by evidence, so B problems CARRY evidence:

- **Format:** 24 problems (prototype-grade N, ~$0.5 to generate). Each = a concealed-
  conflict scenario in a modern real-world setting + a DOSSIER of the counterparty's
  6-8 observed actions/signals over time + the decision now facing the protagonist.
  Generated by DeepSeek (family separation), OOD from all training problems (no pool
  reuse; RI's longitudinal concealed-conflict SHAPE as the format donor, zero fantasy
  content, md5-frozen before judging).
- **Task per arm:** same two-seat harness, same worker prompts as Eval A — no
  arm-specific prompting; the dossier simply arrives as part of the problem text.
- **Judge (Sonnet, new rubric, frozen):** 5 dims, 1-5 — prediction_specificity (names
  the counterparty's next 1-3 moves concretely), evidence_grounding (every prediction
  traceable to a dossier signal, none invented), calibration (confidence scales with
  evidence; prices the quiet-compliance read too), positioning (the plan exploits the
  prediction, not just states it), falsifiability (names observable disconfirming
  signals). overall-B = mean of 5.
- **B BAR (prototype-grade, single session, documented as such):** overall-B(face) ≥
  overall-B(anchor) + 0.20 AND evidence_grounding(face) ≥ anchor (the anti-fantasy leg
  — B must never reward depth that A would call over-reach).
